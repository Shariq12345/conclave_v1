\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

This section reviews existing literature across federated learning frameworks, cryptographic secure aggregation, differential privacy accounting, and Byzantine-resilient aggregation.

\subsection{Federated Learning Frameworks}
Frameworks such as Flower, PySyft, and TensorFlow Federated provide baseline model aggregation capabilities but lack native enterprise governance features, dynamic consent tracking, and audit logging pipelines.

\subsection{Secure Aggregation \& Differential Privacy}
Bonawitz et al.\ introduced pairwise secret sharing for dropout-resilient Secure Aggregation. Mironov extended privacy accounting via Rényi Differential Privacy (RDP). Conclave builds upon these foundations by integrating persistent database budget tracking and $X25519$ key agreement.

\subsection{Byzantine Robustness}
Blanchard et al.\ proposed Krum, while coordinate-wise median and trimmed mean estimators provide resilience against gradient poisoning attacks. Conclave combines robust aggregation with cosine-similarity divergence anomaly detection for automated node isolation.
