\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented Conclave, an enterprise governance control plane for federated learning. By integrating threshold Secure Aggregation, persistent Rényi Differential Privacy budget enforcement, automated HIPAA/GDPR compliance auditing, and Byzantine anomaly detection, Conclave reconciles robust security with high model performance. Future work includes expanding zero-knowledge policy verification and decentralized consortium smart contract audit anchors.
