\section{Cryptography \& Privacy Protocols}
\label{sec:cryptography_privacy}

\subsection{Threshold Secure Aggregation}
Conclave implements an $X25519$ Diffie-Hellman pairwise masking protocol coupled with Shamir's Secret Sharing ($k$-of-$N$). Surviving peers reconstruct dropped client keys to cancel unmatched pairwise masks without exposing active clients' private key material.

\subsection{Rényi Differential Privacy Accounting}
Under the Gaussian mechanism, RDP bound at order $\alpha > 1$ with noise scale $\sigma$ is:
\begin{equation}
    \epsilon_{\text{RDP}}(\alpha) = \frac{\alpha}{2\sigma^2}
\end{equation}
Conclave converts composed RDP guarantees to standard $(\epsilon, \delta)$-DP using Balle et al.'s optimal conversion and enforces cumulative privacy budget ceilings ($\epsilon_{\text{max}}$) per organization.
