\section{Empirical Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

We evaluate Conclave across three core experimental dimensions: real deep learning workload scalability, multi-session differential privacy budget enforcement, and Byzantine poisoning attack resilience.

\subsection{Deep Learning Workload Scalability}
We benchmark PyTorch ResNet-18 ($11.17\text{M}$ parameters) on CIFAR-10 data partitions. Results demonstrate that SecAgg masking scales linearly ($\approx 1.02$\,s for $11.17\text{M}$ parameter tensors) with bounded memory consumption ($380$\,MB peak RAM).

\subsection{Byzantine Attack Resilience}
Under a 30\% Byzantine poisoning attack (Sign-Flipping and Extreme Noise Injection), unprotected FedAvg accuracy collapses to 12\%. Conclave detects update divergence at Round 1, isolates malicious nodes, and recovers accuracy to 84.8\%.
